About

Academic Profile

Dr. Rilwan Abdullahi Aliyu is a dedicated academic and researcher with over 10 years of experience in higher education, having served at Ahmadu Bello University since 2017. His intellectual work bridges theoretical frameworks with real-world political and international dynamics, particularly as they relate to the African continent.

His research engages critical questions about governance, resource distribution, conflict resolution, and Nigeria's role within the broader international order. He brings rigorous scholarship and a commitment to mentoring the next generation of political scientists.

Outside the academy, Dr. Aliyu is an avid reader and a passionate follower of football — interests that sharpen both his analytical thinking and his appreciation for strategy and teamwork.

Areas of Specialization

Core research domains spanning political theory, conflict, and international affairs.

Political Economy

Examining the intersection of political power and economic systems, with emphasis on resource governance and structural inequality in Nigeria and West Africa.

Peace & Conflict Studies

Investigating the causes, dynamics, and resolution mechanisms of armed conflict and post-conflict reconstruction across sub-Saharan Africa.

Foreign Policy

Analysing state behaviour, diplomatic relations, and Nigeria's strategic interests within multilateral frameworks and bilateral engagements.

International Development

Exploring development paradigms, aid architecture, and sustainable strategies for poverty reduction and institutional capacity building.
